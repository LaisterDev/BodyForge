## BepInEx

BodyForge is portable and does not bundle BepInEx in its release archive.

BodyForge requires the community-maintained **BepInExPack for Valheim**. During
first-run setup, and only after the user gives consent, BodyForge downloads the
pinned package directly from Thunderstore and verifies its SHA-256 before
extracting it into the selected Valheim folder.

- Project: BepInEx
- Community pack: BepInExPack Valheim by denikson
- Required core version: 5.4.23.5 or newer
- Recommended package: 5.4.2350
- BepInEx license: LGPL-2.1
- Package page: https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/denikson/BepInExPack_Valheim/
- BepInEx source: https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx

BepInEx and the community package are maintained independently. Their names,
licenses and download locations do not imply endorsement of BodyForge.
